Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Tabby is an expense tracking and bill splitting web app run by an individual developer ("the Tabby developer", "we"). This policy explains what information Tabby collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

From Google sign-in. Tabby only supports signing in with Google. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture URL with Tabby. Tabby never sees or stores your Google password.

What you enter in the app.

  • Your profile: display name, email address and avatar URL.
  • Personal expenses, including amounts, currencies, dates, categories and notes.
  • Groups you create or join, group members, and invitations (including the email addresses you invite).
  • Shared expenses, how they are split, and settlements (payments between people).
  • Friend connections and custom categories.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment card details.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show and calculate your expenses, balances and reports.
  • To let you share expenses with friends and group members, and to identify you to them.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and Tabby contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

3. Sharing and visibility

  • Friends and fellow group members can see your display name, email address and avatar, plus the expenses, splits and settlements you share with them.
  • Finding you by email. A signed-in Tabby user who knows your exact email address can look you up to add you as a friend. There is no public directory or search by partial name or email.
  • Your personal expenses are visible only to you.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

4. Third-party services

  • Google provides sign-in. The app page also loads Google's Identity Services script from accounts.google.com, which means your browser contacts Google when you open Tabby. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase hosts Tabby's authentication and PostgreSQL database and processes your data on our behalf.
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) provides currency exchange rates. Tabby sends it only currency codes, never personal data.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) serves optional trip cover images. When one is shown, your browser makes a standard image request to Unsplash, which includes your IP address and browser details.

5. Google user data

Tabby's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Tabby uses your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create your account and to show who you are to the people you share expenses with. It is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred to others except as described in this policy.

6. Storage and security

  • Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Row Level Security rules in the database limit each record to its owner and the people it is shared with.
  • Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session, theme, default currency and a cached list of currencies.
  • A service worker caches the app's files so it can load offline. It does not cache sign-in tokens or your account data.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Tabby does not yet have an in-app "delete account" button. To delete your account and data, open a request on our GitHub issues page (do not post private details there; we will follow up to verify the request). We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days. Expenses you shared may remain in other members' records where they are needed to keep their balances correct.

8. Your choices

  • Export: on the Settings page you can download your expenses as CSV or a full backup as JSON.
  • Edit: you can change your display name and edit or delete expenses you created.
  • Deletion: request it as described above.
  • Revoke Google access: you can remove Tabby's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

9. Children

Tabby is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has used Tabby, contact us and we will delete their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
